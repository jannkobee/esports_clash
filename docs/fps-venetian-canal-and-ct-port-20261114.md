# Fontessa Venetian canal, CT port and asymmetric sites (14 November 2026)

Owner disliked the river/bridge layout and supplied Venice photographs, requesting an urban canal, tactical crossings, a possible port CT spawn, a claustrophobic B side and a more open A side. This supersedes the previous nine-metre river direction. `/root` owns map/terrain, waterfront/scenery, related regression fixtures and this handoff; no delegates. Other pending edits were preserved.

Map is now `fontessa-village-v8`. Snapshot schema remains v19; no wire fields changed.

- Narrowed the water from 9 m to **5 m** (Z -2.2..2.8). Central south stairs now start at -6.2, giving a compact four-metre approach. Kept the 1 m central deck, gentle stairs, real lower towpath/under-bridge route and apartment stair connection. The removed elevated right branch stays removed.
- Side crossings have short stone arches, coping and admitted low parapets rather than wide unguarded slabs. The western outer crossing narrows to 4 m. The B outer crossing follows the existing raised gallery height instead of rendering an unrelated slab at street grade. Water is impassable outside the crossing footprints.
- Added close-set pastel canal-front houses beyond the arena, waterline footings, shuttered windows, restrained upper balconies, striped mooring poles and two moored boats. Existing north-bank homes frame the playable towpath. New waterfront scenery joins the existing material batching; boats/piles stay in impassable water or outside play bounds.
- Replaced CT's inland rear setting with a **customs port**: tall customs offices flank its dry assembly quay; low masonry reveals harbor water, a landing pier, fenders, navigation poles, police launch and cargo skiff. CT's assembly coordinates remain near (0,41) to preserve setup timing and fanned departures. Removed the north connecting road and north background houses that would sit in the harbor; shared paving cuts remove landscape under the port. The harbor starts at Z 44.5, beyond the Z 43 play boundary.
- B's southern canal house narrows its approach; the taller market row and western residence enclose the fountain neighborhood. A real covered **sottoportego** has its ceiling in shared sight/ballistic collision. B main is 4.9 m wide, versus A main's 6 m. Tall grocer/bakery/rear shops break cross angles. Both entry routes, gallery stairs, fountain plants and rear retake routes remain connected. A's broad civic forecourt and lower approach cover remain.
- Updated one B post-plant position to the legal alley. Reattached festoon cables to the new building faces. The reported-pressure utility fixture now throws from the unobstructed east return because the taller grocer correctly blocks its old trajectory. Learning tests retain full collision/feature checks, permit a single legal action in constrained crossings, and still require multiple alternatives in open A/B pockets. No AI learning, utility or gunplay rules were changed.
