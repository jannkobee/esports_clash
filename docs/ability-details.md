# Spell-specific animation details — 2026-10-17

`EsportsClash.Web/src/threeAbilityDetails.ts` contains the new geometry choreography. `threeAbilityEffects.ts` supplies its existing pooled/instanced drawing primitives and current read-only spell/unit inputs. It adds cached torus-link and curved hook geometry; no external assets or gameplay producers change.

- Hooks have a shaped head, a full caster-to-projectile tether, and alternating chain links with slight sag. Ornament count is capped at 22 regardless of length. Without an available caster, the hook head still renders without guessing an origin.
- All twelve existing dash kinds have motion-specific trails: feathers, stones, flame tongues, electric streaks, leaves or sun/shard accents. Trails use the current unit and recorded dash start, extend at most 76 units backward and never predict a future destination. Missing start data, no motion, expired dash and dead units render no dash trail. Visuals use no position history or match references retained across frames.
- Death Mark draws crossed blades and an orbiting accent; Shallow Grave draws a protective arch. Both follow the live target and disappear on target death. Jaunts and Aether Remnant gain upright portal arcs, with a spectral remnant silhouette for the latter.
- Singularity Well gains accretion bands, inward spiral fragments and drifting debris. Court of Branches has six animated spectral staff motifs around its supplied perimeter; these are decoration, not added units. Cyclone Dance spins a staff and layered crescents around the live caster. Broadside Waltz streams staggered tracers and muzzle flashes within its locked facing and existing forward/lateral boundary. The previous interruption/death/expiry gates remain authoritative.
- Prism Warden gets converging facets at the actual summon time. The live game creates or refreshes it immediately, despite the kit's .25-second delay field, so this formation begins at cast elapsed zero. It does not spawn another model, modify the summon, or postpone its activity. Entangling Claws uses claw sweeps and roots at its actual hit location; the `spirit_bear` hit event is **not** misrepresented as the bear spawning there.

All effects remain functions of simulation time, real positions and active state. They do not mutate borrowed input, consume gameplay RNG, resolve hits, advance simulation, alter movement/range/terrain/targeting, or add resource allocations for geometry every frame. Glow still batches by shared geometry; both added geometry types remain reachable by the existing full disposal traversal. The 2D renderer/fallback, GLB assets, ML candidates/freezes, aliases and protected gameplay rules remain unchanged.
