# FPS Map Scenery Polish (11 November 2026)

The owner supplied a courtyard reference and asked for more map detail. The local Breakwater Yard presentation now gives B a blue, patterned mosaic plaza with a pale stone edge and a low scalloped fountain. The fountain has layered stonework, a translucent pool, a tiered center jet, lily pads and small flowers.

The B-site backdrop now has warm ochre plaster, a timber door, shuttered window, flower box, wall-mounted metal drums and blue barrels staged on the existing Coffins cover. Four larger house blocks have terracotta tiled gable caps. These reuse the current map composition and keep its harbor setting.

All added pieces are visual dressing in `fpsLiveScenery.ts` and `fpsLiveViewer.ts`. No solids were added to `LIVE_FPS_MAP`; collision, sightlines, navigation, bomb-site rules and round behavior are unchanged. The barrels sit on existing cover rather than on a traversable lane.

Automated checks and rendered browser review were not run for this scenery-only pass. The result remains part of the proxy-mesh exhibition and is not a production art asset or visual qualification.
