# Stone courtyards and wetland coves — implementation pending review

Superseded by the user's request to remove the swamp and create forest lowlands around a raised lane. See [forest-ridge-map.md](forest-ridge-map.md). Both revisions remain untested at the user's request; the wetland artwork below is historical.

The user's second annotated map replaces the first swamp design. Blue outlines define two broad wetland regions across the northern and southern jungle. Brown outlines define the stone central lane and the full end bases. The separate terrain reference establishes curved, connected cliff banks, layered rock faces, turf, roots and vegetation as the art direction. All art here is original procedural code.

Implemented source changes:

- Restored staggered stone slabs, bevels, chips, cracks, moss-filled joints and Nexus inlays across the lane/base floors. The lane stays at ground level, with no raised bridge or parapets. `arenaLandform.ts` shares two wetland bounds and slightly irregular lane edges; `threeArenaTerrain.ts` fills the regions with shaded shallow swamp and camp islands. The center tributary through the lane and narrow perimeter-only canal are removed.
- `threeArenaCliffs.ts` builds continuous camp-cove/highground surfaces using sculpted cross sections, ledges, fractures, mineral seams, turf caps and lichen. `threeArenaScenery.ts` replaces the old large standalone boulders with these surfaces, roots, scree, ferns and small bank conifers. Camps gain distinct nests/eggs, bones, fallen logs, mushrooms, crystals and ruined floor remnants. These remain cosmetic, following real bank contours and open entrances; physical terrain/router data is unchanged.
- The cozy end-base courtyards have pitched-roof timber/plaster cabins, arched doors, warm windows, awnings, lanterns, benches, planter flowers, stacked provisions and a striped merchant stall. One small healing lantern/shrine stands at WELL_X, Y322, leaving the Y380 respawn ground open. Giant pools/rims/ellipse outlines are removed from both Three and Canvas. Mechanical healing/shopping/recovery retains its prior 105x240 ellipse at WELL_X, Y380, plus the existing enemy beam boundaries/damage and unsafe-chase rules.
- Every wave class spawns around the home Nexus through the shared helper; claimed siege golems also use the Nexus front. Normal-speed waypoints skirt the middle barracks before joining the lane. Live `components/AramMatchView.tsx` and headless `matchSimulationEngine.ts` use these shared rules. The actual six barracks and their IDs/stats/empowerment remain. Wave timing, unit speed/stats and Nexus seals remain intact.
- The Canvas fallback and minimap use the revised stone/wetland design. An immutable shared background canvas caches slab artwork; only small water glints use match time each frame. Canvas camp walls use continuous strata/roots rather than individual boulder circles. Existing visibility/monster state remains authoritative.
