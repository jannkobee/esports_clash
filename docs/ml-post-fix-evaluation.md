# Live ML data-quality pass (2026-10-15)

The residual macro selector's intended 0.65-second commitment was renewed on every simulation tick. As long as the action context stayed stable, that renewal could keep it committed indefinitely and prevent both ordinary utility reconsideration and a safe randomized comparison. `nextMacroCommitment` now preserves the original deadline while a commitment is being honored. When it expires, the legal selector scores again. Emergency and finish tiers, legality gates, tower-risk combat, normal retreat, and combat mechanics are unchanged.

Evaluation-only assignments now get a snapshot at a fixed three-second follow-up from the live simulation tick, whether or not the actor makes another sampled macro decision. The optional browser fixture accepts a validated central-lane geometry and separate seeded assignment RNG before the match begins. Ordinary matches have neither fixture enabled. Six fresh 35-second scenario episodes (seeds 81–86) produced one assignment with one outcome; these staged starts are too sparse to train from. See `ml-scenario-evaluation.json`. They do not establish live/headless parity or a gameplay advantage.

The first seed-74 natural probe overlapped source edits that could trigger browser hot reload, so its 14 apparent assignments and any fit using it are **invalidated**. Six clean natural browser matches (seed 74 original, seed 75 swapped, seeds 77 and 78 on both sides) produced 19 assignments and 19 valid follow-ups. Per-run assignment counts were 2, 4, 2, 5, 0, and 6. The regroup-incumbent/fight-alternate stratum had four and five valid outcomes: mean progress per second 0.05245 versus 0.02401, alternate-minus-incumbent -0.02843, descriptive 95% half-width 0.08736. The interval includes zero and ignores actor/match interference. Other action strata did not meet the existing three-per-arm fit threshold. Clean sources are `ml-post-fix-probe-74.json`, `ml-post-fix-probe-75.json`, `ml-post-fix-probe-77-78-both.json`, and `ml-post-fix-analysis.json`; `ml-post-fix-probe.json` is retained only as the invalidated diagnostic.

Any fit changes only a small offline action bias. It does not estimate a card-specific causal effect, change card combat stats, widen the live learned utility weight, or make an unavailable action legal. The existing live card-aware winner-trained policy continues separately. A three-second health/gold/CS/damage proxy is not the same as winning a match.

Held-out seed 79 ran with the offline candidate executed and the incumbent scored against the exact same legal candidates and commitment on both side assignments. The candidate changed **zero of 12,894** same-state choices (5,620 original, 7,274 swapped), including zero suppressed-state changes. Its learned weight had no demonstrated behavioral or gameplay benefit, so the candidate was **not promoted**. See `ml-post-fix-heldout.json`. Replay durations and winners cannot attribute a benefit when the compared policies selected the same action in these samples.
