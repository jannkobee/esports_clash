## Financial correctness

ClubFinances uses a repeatable-read, read-only reporting transaction. ClubFinanceController provisions the authenticated club and initializes its normal opening ledger only if missing before reporting. No payment, prize, payroll or transaction is invented by the report. Capital is excluded from revenue. Auction/trade reservations and releases are excluded from income/expenses; winning purchases and offered trade cash are recognized once at actual settlement. The ledger remains the original available-money movement history, so escrow explains why its cash movement differs from the operating statement. Requested trade cash is already debited at settlement and is not counted a second time. No retroactive ledger rewrite or contract repricing.

Payroll cover is based on actual available money and current weekly player/staff/youth-coach costs. It is a budget projection, not a fabricated accrued-liability report or assumed revenue forecast; expiring contracts and roster changes can alter charges.

## Staff offer pricing v2

Let q be the mean of the four staff attributes plus 15% of the gap between their maximum and mean. Weekly salary is PHP `80 + 12*q^2 + 25*max(0,q-6)^3`, rounded to the nearest PHP10. S tier begins at q 8.5; A at 7.5, B at 6.5, otherwise C. Perfect q10 staff cost PHP2,880/week versus the former PHP200. An eight-week appointment costs ten salaries including the two-week signing fee. Candidate IDs include `v2` so an old cached offer cannot be silently accepted at a new price. Actual signed contracts and founding arrangements retain their original terms. Hire validation still requires four weeks of payroll available after signing; bots use their existing conservative affordability checks.

## Form model v1

PlayerForm is a server-owned read projection used by both squad and Hexapedia career records. It uses up to ten verified individual ratings in the preceding 56 days. For appearance i, newest first, weight is `2^(-ageDays/21) * 0.85^i`; form is `(6*2 + sum(weight*rating))/(2 + sum(weight))`, rounded to one decimal and bounded 1–10. Two neutral prior appearances temper extreme small samples. With no recent samples, score is null and the UI says Unrated, rather than displaying an earned 6.0. Legacy recent_form_rating now projects that same canonical value.

Evidence labels describe sample count: Provisional below three appearances, Building below five, Established thereafter. They are not statistical confidence percentages. A rising/falling trend compares the latest three ratings with the preceding three and requires six samples, using a 0.25 threshold. W/L chips are separate team results. Historical match-rating rows are unchanged. Form does not modify combat stats, player attributes, training growth, mechanical legality or the learned controller. New community-fun matches intentionally produce no individual rating rows.
