## Compute and scheduling

The existing low-priority coordinator runs once per five-minute slot on the shared persistent default/world worker. It reviews at most eight clubs with an eight-second cooperative budget and a 25-second job timeout. Careers share that budget with a two-second substage: at most three negotiations, two delegations, two vacancies, two trade responses, two person reviews, one due club career review and eight account admissions. Career club reviews are due daily; normal management reviews remain staggered. Individual atomic transactions can finish after the soft deadline. Core queue pressure and allocated/running matches defer optional work. There is no per-person process, per-club recurring fan-out, model call or GPU task.

Binding obligations run through the existing five-minute market command even when optional management is paused or retired. Social posting remains bounded and evidence-linked. Text is composed from finite authored grammar; this is adaptive simulation, not unlimited generated prose. No API key or paid provider is needed.
