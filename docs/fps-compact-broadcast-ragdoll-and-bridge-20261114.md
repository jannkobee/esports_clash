# Compact broadcast controls, impact ragdolls and apartment crossing (14 November 2026)

## Request and ownership

Owner requested F fullscreen and Space pause/resume shortcuts, death reactions based on the fatal hit (headshots should lead with the head), a shorter HUD that does not cover the gun, and a continuous raised connection from the bridge's right side to the apartment. `/root` owns this pass; no delegates. The standing skip-tests/checks instruction remains active.

## Implementation

- **Keyboard and fullscreen:** document-level listeners in the main tab and Document PIP keep F/Space working after clicking player cards, camera controls or toolbar buttons. Editing fields, composing text, browser modifier shortcuts and key repeat are excluded. Pause updates the simulation control ref immediately and respects readiness/error/finished guards. F uses the visible broadcast element and its owning document; Escape follows the browser's native exit. Added a visible fullscreen button, shortcut hints and synchronized fullscreen state. Preserved C camera, P plant PIP and 1-0 player selection. Listeners clean up when documents/component state change.
- **Clear camera picture:** the stage is now a camera-view plus a separate 100 px footer. The WebGL canvas and all its overlays share the camera-view dimensions; the ten-player dock no longer overlays the gun or moves the DOM crosshair away from the camera centre. Existing resize observers resize the renderer/PIP viewport. Player cards and observed-player detail are fixed/compact (88 px); KDA, HP, money, equipment, utility, ammo, action, enemy count and camera controls remain. Detailed intent, radio and attribute notes move below the stage, with hover details on the focus card. On narrow screens each force's five-player strip scrolls horizontally while CT stays left and T right. Organizer palettes/shapes remain distinct. Fullscreen fills the available height and hides below-stage notes/impact tables; Document PIP HUD hiding also hides the footer and releases that space.
- **Impact-aware bodies:** fatal snapshots carry cosmetic hit region, world hit point, direction and bounded strength, plus actual horizontal/vertical movement at death. Rifle/pistol/sniper rays use the actual hit position and shot direction. Lower-body hits distinguish legs from torso; HE/C4 use the blast origin; fire gets a separate collapse. Headshots seed a head/neck-led directional fall, torso hits fold the chest/hips, legs buckle and blasts lift/rotate the body. Off-centre impact and weighted joint constraints shape the response. The head follows its neck segment rather than copying the torso angle. Existing crouch proportions, gravity, stairs/deck/arch collision, floor selection and borrowed-rig reuse remain; corpse stepping follows simulation time, so pause freezes it. Impact metadata is deep-copied into snapshots and never enters AI perception, targeting or damage. These remain cosmetic runtime proxy bodies, not qualified production animation assets.
- **Bridge-to-apartment route:** the suspended 1.8 m-high crossing now spans X 1.6..30.4 / Z 3.2..5.4 and meets the existing apartment promenade at X 30.4..33.4. Added continuous paving, structural beams and additional supports. South/north guardrails follow the extended deck; the north market stair mouth remains open. Split the apartment edge parapet around Z 2.9..5.7, making an actual traversable connection. The lower riverbank and apartment stair route remain open below. Relocated the old market bin to street grade at (27.2,11.4) instead of leaving it over the depressed route. Movement, navigation, bullets, grenades and rendered geometry use the shared deck/support/rail volumes.

Map is `fontessa-village-v6`; local snapshots are `hexatrick-live-exhibition-v18`. Owned source: `FpsLiveBroadcast.tsx`, `fps-live-broadcast.css`, `fpsLiveTerrain.ts`, `fpsLiveMap.ts`, `fpsVillageDesign.ts`, `fpsLiveScenery.ts`, `fpsLiveSimulation.ts`, `fpsLiveRagdoll.ts` and `fpsLiveViewer.ts`.
